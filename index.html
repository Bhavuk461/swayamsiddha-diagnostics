01 About the centre

Precision you can check.
Care you can feel.

Swayamsiddha Diagnostics was set up on a simple idea: a small town deserves the same instrument quality a city hospital runs on. Every routine parameter here is measured on a closed, automated analyzer — the sample goes in, the machine pipettes, incubates, reads and prints. Fewer hands on the sample means fewer chances to get it wrong.

  • Automation firstChemistry, haematology and immunoassay all run on closed automated systems with on-board quality control.
  • DR, not CROur X-ray uses a direct flat-panel detector — the image appears in seconds, at a lower radiation dose.
  • Reports that travelCollect in person or get a copy on WhatsApp. Most routine reports are ready the same day.
  • Straight pricingRates are told upfront at the counter. No package you did not ask for.

03 How it works

From the front door
to your report.

Four steps, no surprises. This is exactly what happens to your sample between walking in and reading the result.

  1. 01

    Walk in or call ahead

    No appointment needed for routine tests. Bring your prescription if you have one, and ask the rate at the counter before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Sample taken

    A fresh needle and a sealed vacutainer, opened in front of you. X-ray and ECG are done in the same visit — no second trip.

  3. 03

    Run on the analyzer

    Your sample goes straight onto a closed automated system. Daily controls run alongside it, so the numbers are checked before they reach you.

  4. 04

    Report the same day

    Most routine reports are ready the same day. Collect at the counter, or ask us to send it to you on WhatsApp.

Radiology

DR digital X-ray

Direct radiography with a flat-panel detector. No cassette, no plate to carry to a scanner — the exposure lands straight on the detector and the image is on screen in seconds.

Detector
Direct flat-panel — no cassette handling or plate erasing
Radiation dose
Lower than CR at equal image quality, thanks to far higher X-ray conversion efficiency
Speed
Image available within seconds of exposure
Post-processing
Windowing, zoom and contrast adjustment on the digital image

Why we insisted on DR over CR

Both are “digital X-ray”, and plenty of centres let you assume they are the same thing. They are not.

CR — computed radiography

  • Exposure is stored on a cassette plate
  • The plate is carried to a reader and scanned
  • Image appears after the scan and erase cycle
  • X-ray conversion efficiency of roughly 20–35%
  • A repeat means the whole cassette cycle again

DR — what we run

  • Exposure is captured by the flat-panel detector itself
  • Nothing to carry, scan or erase
  • Image on screen in seconds
  • Conversion efficiency well beyond CR — so a lower dose
  • A repeat view takes seconds, not minutes

Lower dose and fewer retakes matter most for the people we X-ray most often — children, pregnant patients and the elderly.
